# ✍️ The chosen questions

108 questions about Magic: The Gathering cards, chosen and ranked by me before TopDeck's graph had a layout.

## Why these questions

I want TopDeck to be mainly about questions that can't easily be answered with a web search. When I rated the drafts, the ones I rated highest shared three things:

- **They need judgement or a story,** not one fact from a table: rules interactions, advice on a deck, "what happened to" and "why".
- **Art, artists and flavour text, with a twist:** art that joins up, a different artist on every reprint, cards that care about the art.
- **"Does anything like this exist?" in an odd corner:** a colour doing what it shouldn't, an unlikely pairing, a one-of-a-kind card.

Many of them can't be answered from card data at all: what is painted in a picture, why a rule was removed, what to cut from a deck. That is on purpose. For those, the test is whether a system says it can't answer, or answers with a source a reader can check, rather than inventing something.

## How they were made

Most of TopDeck's questions are built from templates, and a template is shaped by the tables it will be answered from. These are the check on that: they were fixed before there were any tables to shape them, and the Git history shows they came first.

- **First,** two model helpers drafted 132 questions, one playing a kitchen-table player and one a deck builder. Neither had seen the graph's layout.
- **Then** I rated every draft from one mark to five, or crossed it out.
- **I kept** the 72 rated five, four and three, and dropped the rest.
- **Last,** a third helper, which saw only my ratings, wrote 45 more in the spirit of my favourites. I kept 36.

The ranks below are mine. Once a result has been seen, no question on this page is edited.

`uv run topdeck ground` counts them and checks each one is well formed.
